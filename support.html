Daybezel — Support

Daybezel is a free alarm clock for iPhone that shows the next 24 hours as one dial. It is made by one person, Oleksandr Vovchuk, in Ukraine.

Contact

Email wishu.developer@gmail.com and say which iPhone and iOS version you have. You will get an answer from Alex himself, usually within two days.

Common questions

An alarm did not ring. Daybezel schedules alarms with Apple’s AlarmKit, the system the built-in Clock uses, so they ring with the app closed, the phone locked, in Silent mode and through a Focus. Two things can stop that: Daybezel’s alarm permission is off (iPhone Settings → Daybezel → Alarms, turn it on; the dial shows a blocked state and a button when this is the case), or the phone was powered off at the alarm time (a phone that is off cannot ring; this is true for every alarm app). If neither applies, please email with the time of the alarm and what the dial showed afterwards.

How do I move an alarm? Drag its pebble along the ring; the bar and the countdown follow. It moves in five-minute steps, and in one-minute steps within half an hour of now. For exact minutes, tap the pebble, then tap the time on the card that opens.

How do I delete an alarm? Swipe its row in the list to the left, or tap the pebble and tap the red “Delete” at the top left of its card, or drag the pebble into the centre of the dial. Every delete can be undone from the message that appears.

What are rings? One alarm can ring several times a few minutes apart, for example 07:00, 07:05, 07:10 and 07:15. It is still one alarm on the dial, with small beads after it. Stop ends the ring that is sounding; the later rings still come. To end all of them, open Daybezel and tap “I’m up” on its ringing screen.

Why do I not see tomorrow’s alarms? The dial always shows the next 24 hours from now. An alarm further away appears when it comes within that window; until then a repeating alarm is in the list under the dial with the weekday it rings next. Repeating alarms keep ringing even if you never open the app.

Does Daybezel send my data anywhere? No. There is no account, no server and no analytics. Your alarms are stored on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.

Which iPhones and iOS versions? iPhone with iOS 26.1 or later. AlarmKit does not exist before iOS 26, and iOS 26.0 has known problems with alarm sounds, so 26.1 is the minimum. iPad is not supported yet.

Can I use my own sound? Version 1 ships with fifteen alarm sounds of Daybezel’s own, plus Apple’s standard alarm sound. Importing your own files is on the list for a later version.

Reporting a problem

Email the steps you took, what you expected and what happened, plus a screenshot if there is one. For a missed alarm, say the time it was set for, whether it repeats or rings several times, and what the dial showed afterwards. Settings → Support in the app opens an email with the app’s version already filled in.
