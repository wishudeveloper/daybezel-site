Daybezel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Daybezel is an alarm clock for iPhone made by Alex Vovchuk (“we”, which is one person). This policy says what the app does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your phone, and we never see it.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your alarms: the time, the name you gave each one (for example “Wake up”), how many times it rings, whether it repeats, and the sound and snooze settings. They are saved in a file inside the app’s private storage on your iPhone. If the app’s widget is on, a copy is kept in the app’s own shared folder on the same phone so the widget can read it.
  • Your settings: default sound, snooze length, haptics and the dial’s orientation. They are saved in the app’s preferences on your iPhone.
  • An activity log: when an alarm was scheduled, rang, was stopped or snoozed, and any scheduling error. It is a plain text file on your iPhone, kept so that you can see what happened and send it to us if you choose to.

Nothing on this list leaves your phone. iCloud backup of your phone may include the app’s files like any other app’s; that is Apple’s backup, under your Apple account and your control.

What we collect

Nothing. Daybezel has no account, no sign-in, no server and no network connection. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no third-party software development kits. We cannot see your alarms, your names for them, or when you use the app. The App Store listing says “Data Not Collected”, and that is the whole truth.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Alarms (AlarmKit). Daybezel asks iOS for permission to schedule alarms, so they can ring even when the app is closed or the phone is in Silent mode. iOS shows the request the first time you add an alarm. You can change your answer at any time in iPhone Settings → Daybezel. Without it, the app can draw the dial but cannot ring.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or calendar.

Sharing

The app does not share data with anyone, because it has none to share. If you choose to email us the activity log from the Support page, you send it yourself from your own Mail app; we use it only to help you and delete it when the problem is solved.

Deleting your data

Delete an alarm in the app and it is gone from the file. Delete the app and iOS removes everything it stored, including the log and the widget’s copy. There is nothing on our side to delete.

Children

Daybezel is rated 4+ and contains nothing unsuitable for children, but it is not designed for them and does not knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If a future version ever adds something that collects or sends data (for example an optional sync), this page will say so before that version is released, and the App Store privacy label will be updated with it.
