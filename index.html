Daybezel

Your next day on one dial.

Daybezel is an alarm clock for iPhone that shows you the next 24 hours as one dial. Every alarm is a pebble on the ring, and one orange bar runs from now to the next alarm, so you see the wait before you read a number. Free, no account, everything stays on your phone.
